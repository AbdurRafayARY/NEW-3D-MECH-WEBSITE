About 3D Mech Design

Engineering the products that move the world.

Founded in 2007, 3D Mech Design has grown from a two-person CAD studio into a global mechanical engineering partner serving some of the most demanding industries on earth — aerospace, automotive, energy, medical devices, and advanced manufacturing.

We combine deep domain expertise with modern simulation, digital twin, and Industry 4.0 capabilities. Every engagement is led by senior engineers, documented to audit-ready standards, and delivered with measurable outcomes — not just deliverables.

Senior-led teams

Every project has a principal engineer accountable for outcomes.

Global delivery

How We Work

A disciplined, transparent engineering process

Every engagement follows a proven six-stage methodology — with clear deliverables, gates, and metrics at each step.

01

Discover

Requirements capture, stakeholder interviews, and feasibility study.

02

Concept

Trade studies, concept modeling, and early validation.

03

Design

Detailed CAD, GD&T, DFM/DFA, and drawing release.

04

Simulate

FEA, CFD, thermal, and durability analysis to validate.

05

Validate

Prototype, physical test, correlation, and design freeze.

06

Deliver

Production release, PLM handover, and manufacturing support.
